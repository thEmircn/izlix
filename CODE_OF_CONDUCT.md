# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 2.1: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and keep discussion focused on the work.
Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated. Report concerns privately through GitHub's "Report content" feature or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
